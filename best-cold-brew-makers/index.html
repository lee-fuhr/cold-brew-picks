The best cold brew makers of 2026

Updated June 2026Independently researchedNo paid placement.

Quick answer

The Takeya Cold Brew Coffee Maker is our top pick for 2026. it’s the most popular for good reason, combining simplicity, durability, and a smooth result at a very fair price.

If you want a more refined brewing system, the OXO Good Grips delivers exceptional clarity and strength; for budget shoppers, the Hario Mizudashi offers a compact, elegant solution; and the Toddy remains the editor's choice for serious concentrate lovers.

Cold brew isn’t just a summer drink anymore, it’s a year-round ritual for anyone who craves a smoother, less acidic coffee without the hassle of heat. A dedicated cold brew maker removes the guesswork: you get a consistent concentrate that keeps for up to two weeks, saving you time and money versus café versions. The key choices come down to batch size, filter type (fine mesh vs. paper felt), and whether you prefer a carafe or a jar for storage. When picking your cold brew maker, think about how much you drink and how strong you like it. A simpler glass pitcher with a mesh filter (like the Takeya or Hario) is great for small households and easy cleanup. Larger systems with a separate brew-and-store design (like the OXO or Toddy) give you more control over strength and yield, but take up more counter space. We focused on models with solid owner feedback, reliable construction, and a track record of producing clean, sediment-free concentrate without bitterness.
