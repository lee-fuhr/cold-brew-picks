How we choose our picks

We don’t run a lab. Instead, we spend hours reading owner reviews across retail sites, discussion forums like r/coldbrew, and expert roundups from sources such as America’s Test Kitchen and Serious Eats. We look for patterns in both praise and complaints, not just star ratings but what actually holds up over months of daily use. Durability is our top concern. The most common failure points are cracked carafes, leaky lid gaskets, and fine‑mesh strainers that corrode or clog beyond repair. We also prioritize clear‑glass or BPA‑free plastic carafes that fit on a refrigerator door shelf. A cold brew maker that doesn’t fit in the fridge or is a pain to clean won’t get used, no matter how good the concentrate tastes.
